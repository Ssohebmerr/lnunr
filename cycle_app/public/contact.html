Contact Us

We're here to help — support, privacy, and billing.

Whether you need help with the app, have a privacy question, or want to report a problem — reach out. We read every message and aim to reply within a few business days (privacy requests within 30 days as required by law).

In-app: Account → Privacy Center → Contact Support for the fastest path. You can also email us directly below.

Email merrsoheb@gmail.com

  • General support

    merrsoheb@gmail.com
    App issues, feature questions, feedback.

  • Privacy & data requests

    merrsoheb@gmail.com
    Access, deletion, or questions about your data. You can also use Contact Support in the app's Privacy Center.

  • Premium & billing

    Subscriptions are managed through Google Play. To cancel or request a refund, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. For activation issues in Lune, try Account → Restore purchases in the app, then email us if needed.

  • Partnerships

    Content, education, or collaboration inquiries: Partners page or merrsoheb@gmail.com

Response times

General support

Usually 2–5 business days.

Privacy requests

Within 30 days as required by applicable law.

Billing (Google Play)

Refunds and cancellations via Google Play first; we help with activation issues.

Partnerships

5–10 business days for collaboration inquiries.
