# Lune — Moon Brand Guide

**Lune** means *moon* in French. The brand rests on one natural metaphor: **the moon cycles through phases, and so does the female body.** Use this everywhere — website, Play Store, social, photography, and in-app copy — without making it mystical or religious. Keep it calm, feminine, and private.

## Core message

| Layer | English | Arabic |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Name** | Lune | Lune (لوِن) |
| **Tagline** | Your cycle has phases — like the moon. | دورتكِ لها أطوار — كالقمر. |
| **Promise** | A private moon-phase wellness companion. | رفيقتكِ الخاصة لعافية دورتك، بهدوء القمر. |
| **One-liner** | Lune means moon — your cycle has phases, just like the sky. | Lune تعني القمر — لدورةٍ أطوار كأطوار السماء. |

## Visual identity

- **Logo:** Crescent + soft teardrop/orbit on rose → lavender gradient (`tool/gen_app_icon.py`)
- **Palette:** Rose `#E8A0BF`, lavender `#C9B8E8`, cream `#FFFBF8`, plum `#5C3D52`
- **Moon motif:** Thin crescent, soft glow, circular “phase ring” (cycle UI)
- **Avoid:** Stock “full moon” clichés, zodiac, witchy imagery, overly dark night skies

## Photography & video

### Mood
- Soft natural light, dusk/dawn, window light, calm bedroom or living room
- Models: diverse women 18+, relaxed, not posed like fashion ads
- **Props:** subtle moon — crescent lamp, night sky through window, reflection on water, circular mirror echoing the cycle ring

### App in frame
- Show **home screen with cycle ring** as hero shot
- Device on soft blush/lavender surface; optional faint crescent bokeh in background
- Caption overlays (social): tagline in plum on cream, or white on rose gradient

### Play Store & website screenshots
1. Home (cycle ring + phase) — caption: *أطوار دورتكِ، بهدوء*
2. Daily log — *سجّلي يومكِ بلطف*
3. Analytics — *افهمي أنماطكِ*
4. AI — *همسة Lune* (Premium)
5. Self-care — *رعاية هادئة*
6. Privacy — *خصوصيتكِ كالقمر — بعيدة وآمنة*

### Feature graphic (1024×500)
- Left: Lune crescent logo
- Center: **دورتكِ لها أطوار — كالقمر** / *Your cycle has phases — like the moon.*
- Background: rose → lavender gradient mesh (match `site.css`)

## Copy vocabulary

### Use
| EN | AR |
|----|-----|
| phases | أطوار |
| gentle / quiet | هادئ / بهدوء |
| whisper | همسة |
| rhythm | إيقاع |
| waxing / waning (light) | تزايد / تناقص (خفيف) |
| lunar | قمري |
| companion | رفيقة |

### Avoid
- Competitor names (Flo, etc.)
- “Magic moon power” or medical claims tied to lunar cycles
- Overusing emoji 🌙 in product UI (OK sparingly in social)
